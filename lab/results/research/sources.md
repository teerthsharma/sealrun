- MtSharpGrain wiki ([WorldGeneration](https://github.com/OxillenGlow/MtSharpGrain/wiki/WorldGeneration), last edit 2026-09-08): randomness "used as the single source of randomness", "ensuring 100% order-independent and reproducible" generation. A design claim with no proof or count. (search ("arxiv Minecraft world generation determinism order dependent chunks formal model procedural generation seed analysis"))
- Adrenaline (see A4): a footprint scheduler that claims vanilla output with parallel order. Developer claim. [Adrenaline on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/ZOetjTKq) (search ("Adrenaline Minecraft chunk generation footprint parallel scheduler same output as vanilla"))
- Nearest formal analogue with a count: Macauley, McCammond and Mortveit, "Order Independence in Asynchronous Cellular Automata", J. Cellular Automata 3(1) 2008, arXiv [0707.2360](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2360). Abstract (fetch extraction): an ACA is pi-independent if its periodic states do not depend on the permutation; for n > 3, 104 of 256 Wolfram rules are pi-independent. This counts rules, not generation orders. (search ("Fates Schönfisch de Roos asynchronous cellular automata order independence confluence 1999 abstract"))
- Schönfisch and de Roos, "Synchronous and asynchronous updating in cellular automata", BioSystems 51 (1999) 123-143, per a listing on [dare.uva.nl](https://dare.uva.nl/id/d0fa09ce-df84-48b6-8a04-44ca7f6cdcb3) (abstract summary from search, not fetched). Stationary states stay the same across the updating schemes. Not a count of orders. (query: Schönfisch de Roos "Synchronous and asynchronous updating in cellular automata" BioSystems 1999)

## C. Mathematics

### C1. Trace monoids and acyclic orientations

- Trace monoid: [Wikipedia Trace monoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_monoid) (fetch extraction; page fetched directly). "Traces were introduced by Pierre Cartier and Dominique Foata in 1969." And: "sets of commuting letters are given by an independency relation." The page also says trace monoids are isomorphic to the monoid of dependency graphs. (query: Cartier Foata trace monoid acyclic orientations dependence graph each letter once bijection Diekert Book of Traces)
- Stanley 1973: [MathWorld Acyclic orientation](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/AcyclicOrientation.html) (fetch extraction): "the number of acyclic orientations is (-1)^n pi_G(-1)," citing Stanley, Disc. Math. 5, 171-178 (1973). This is the chromatic-polynomial-at-minus-one identity for acyclic orientations. (query: Stanley "Acyclic orientations of graphs" 1973 Discrete Mathematics chromatic polynomial -1 Euler measure)
- Link between traces and acyclic orientations: NOT FOUND in any fetched source ([Wikipedia Trace monoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_monoid); [arXiv 1506.02455 HTML](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1506.02455)). Two searches did not reach the Diekert text. My own derivation, to be checked in Diekert and Rozenberg, *The Book of Traces* (1995) or Cartier and Foata (1969): take a graph G with one vertex per chunk and an edge between chunks that interact. Two orderings of the chunks give the same world iff they induce the same orientation on every edge. The classes are then the acyclic orientations, and their count is |chi_G(-1)| by Stanley. This is a hypothesis, not a source claim. (query: Cartier Foata trace monoid acyclic orientations dependence graph each letter once bijection Diekert Book of Traces; query: trace monoid words where each letter occurs once equivalence classes correspond acyclic orientations dependence graph Stanley 1973)
- Uniform generation in trace monoids, [arXiv 1506.02455](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1506.02455) (HTML fetch, Section 3): the Mobius polynomial is a signed sum over cliques of the independence graph, and it relates to the growth series of traces. This counts traces by clique structure, not by chromatic polynomial or acyclic orientation. It is the nearest counting tool for traces found in this run. (search ("procedural generation partially commutative trace monoid order independence local rewriting confluence"))

### C3. Postnikov, hypergraphic polytopes, nestohedra (new question added by coordinator)

Question: does any paper connect (i) the image of the map from linear orders to the first element of each set in a family with (ii) vertices of generalized permutohedra or nestohedra?
